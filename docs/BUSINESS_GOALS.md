# Business Goals — EKOS Mimoň Variant B

## Primary Business Goal

Generate qualified business enquiries.

## Secondary Business Goal

Enable direct phone contact with minimal friction.

## Conversions

- Primary conversion: submission of a qualified enquiry
- Secondary conversion: phone contact
- Supporting micro-conversions:
  - opening the contact page,
  - viewing relevant capabilities or services,
  - reviewing credibility evidence,
  - initiating an email or phone action.

## CTA Principles

- CTA wording must be specific and relevant to the visitor's decision stage.
- The primary CTA should lead to an enquiry path.
- The phone option must remain clear and accessible.
- Do not overload every section with the same high-intensity CTA.

## Success Criteria

| Criterion | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Value proposition clarity | Understandable quickly | User review / usability review |
| Enquiry path | Functional and low-friction | Manual end-to-end test |
| Phone path | Visible and functional | Manual device test |
| Credibility | Supported by verified evidence | Content-source review |
| Responsive conversion | Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile | QA checklist |

## Open Questions

- Define analytics and measurable conversion targets.
- Confirm the required enquiry form fields.
- Confirm the phone number and contact ownership from an approved source.
